About me

My specialty? Complex SaaS & B2B products — turning friction-heavy, data-dense experiences into intuitive, high-performing, inclusive journeys.

I pair measurable business impact with design leverage: AI-augmented workflows, scalable design systems, and research practices that make the whole team faster, not just screen delivery.

If a picture is worth 1000 words, a prototype is worth 1000 meetings.

— Tom & David Kelley

I measure my impact by the speed and trust the whole team gains, not just the visual polish of an interface.

  • Growing the team's autonomy

    When design becomes a mandatory checkpoint, I train PMs, developers, marketing and product engineers to work directly in the design system and run their own user interviews. At Dualsun, that unblocked the team without hiring.

    −50% research time

  • Moving faster with AI, without losing control

    With Gabriela, a product designer at CoderPad, we linked a tokenized design system, Claude Design and Claude Code into an end-to-end pipeline, to ship faster without cutting corners: I led the design system and accessibility, she led the Claude Code ↔ Figma connection. And when I design AI features, a human always keeps the final call — never the algorithm alone.

    −81% design → review cycle

  • Building accessible foundations from day one

    I bake accessibility (WCAG, RGAA) and eco-design (RGESN) into the design system's foundations, not as an afterthought — for products more people can use, with less debt to fix later.

    WCAG-compliant design systems, at scale

  • Growing with the team

    At CoderPad, I ran a weekly 1:1 with each of the two other designers and facilitated the biweekly Design Reviews. At Dualsun, I directed two freelance designers: briefs, quality feedback, and folding their work into the design system. I learn from others too: Gabriela taught me how to connect Claude Code and Figma, and Laura White, Dualsun's PM, taught me roadmapping and benchmarking.

    2 freelancers directed · 2 designers coached · Design Reviews led
